# Brix Prediction: 2021 to 2022

## Conclusion

The ID-matched 2021-to-2022 GBLUP experiment reached a mean predictive ability of **0.3327**, close to the paper's reported value of approximately **0.35** for its 2021 training set.

This is lower than the reproduced 2022 within-year baseline of 0.4024. The decrease is expected because measurements from a different year add environmental variation.

## Alignment Correction

The upstream script builds the 2021 training table and 2022 evaluation table separately, then compares them by row position. The two years do not contain exactly the same samples, so row positions can refer to different plants.

This reproduction explicitly matches both years by `germplasmName` before splitting. A total of 1,019 plants have 2021 Brix, 2022 Brix, and SNP data.
